Daily Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026 · Developer: Urban Monk · App: Daily Tracker (com.urbanmonk.dailytracker)

Daily Tracker is a habit and task tracker. We built it so your data stays on your phone. This policy explains what the app reads, what it sends, and why.

What stays on your phone

Your tasks, history, goals, scribbles and settings are stored only on your device. We don't run servers that store your data, and the app has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash-reporting tools.

Health data

If you connect Health Connect (where Google Fit and Samsung Health store data), Daily Tracker reads the following to fill in your tasks and goals:

Data readUsed for
StepsStep-goal tasks, such as "10,000 steps"
SleepSleep tasks, such as "Sleep 7 hours"
ExerciseWorkout-minute tasks
HydrationWater-intake tasks
Active caloriesCalorie-burn tasks
WeightWeight goals and their trend chart

The app can also read this data in the background, so tasks and the home-screen widget stay up to date while the app is closed. Health data is read and used on your device only. It never leaves your device, and it is not shared, sold, or used for advertising. Our use of data from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Phone usage

If you allow usage access, the app reads how long you've used your phone, or an app you choose, today. It uses this to fill in screen-time, app-time and "no phone after" tasks. This data stays on your device.

AI features

The assistant and the daily encouraging line use an AI service. When they run, the app sends text over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to OpenRouter, which passes it to an AI model provider to write a reply.

  • Assistant: the sentence you type, today's date, and the tasks you added by hand (their titles, whether each is done, and counts you've logged).
  • Encouraging line: how many tasks you've done today, your streak, and the titles of unfinished tasks you added by hand.

Tasks that fill in automatically from Health Connect or phone usage are never included, by title or by status, and neither are your health or phone-usage numbers. They only count towards the totals in the encouraging line. The app doesn't store this text anywhere off your phone. OpenRouter and the model provider process it to return a response, and their own privacy policies apply to it.

Sharing

We don't sell your data or share it with anyone for advertising. The only data that leaves your phone is the AI text described above, sent to the service providers that write the reply.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your phone. Clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app, to delete all of it. You can also delete any task inside the app at any time. You can revoke Health Connect or usage access in Android settings whenever you like.

Children

Daily Tracker is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
